Lab test: the double-count problem

I built a Shopify dev store wired to server-side GTM, GA4 and the Meta Conversions API, then ran test orders through it. This is a controlled lab test, not client data.

Controlled Test on Shopify Dev Store (Lab Test) · 20 orders
5 of 20
orders double-fired on Meta before the fix (thank-you page reload)
3 of 20
orders never reached GA4
0 of 20
duplicates after the fix, with Meta EMQ at 8.8 / 10

The fix: pass order_id as the event_id on both the browser and server events, and route the orders/paid webhook server-side, so Meta can deduplicate the two.

20-order sample on a dev store. Raw counts shown; not a claim about any client's results.

Where else tracking leaks

The thank-you page is only one place. A tracking audit also checks these, because they sit outside the standard checkout flow:

  • ✓ Custom /pages/ advertorials and landing pages that skip the standard theme events
  • ✓ Bundle builders and cart drawers that add items without firing AddToCart
  • ✓ ViewContent and AddToCart payloads missing value, currency or content IDs
  • ✓ Thank-you page reloads and back-button replays that fire Purchase twice
